\section{What Was Done This Week}

\subsection{Navigation Consistency Review}

The \texttt{Navbar.tsx} component built by Role~5 was reviewed against the
approved navigation model from Week~2. Four label mismatches were identified
that must be corrected before the Navbar is used in any visual QA or user
testing.

\vspace{0.8em}
\noindent\textbf{Required label corrections:}
\vspace{0.4em}

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{3.2cm}L{3.8cm}L{2.0cm}L{4.2cm}@{}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{uvtLightBlue}
\textbf{Current label} & \textbf{Correct label} & \textbf{Severity} & \textbf{Route} \\
\midrule
\endhead
About & \textbf{About DRI} & Medium & \path{/about/} \\
\addlinespace
Admissions & \textbf{International Students} & Critical & \path{/international-students/} \\
\addlinespace
Erasmus & \textbf{Erasmus+} & High & \path{/erasmus/} \\
\addlinespace
Research & \textbf{Partnerships} & Critical & \path{/partnerships/} \\
\addlinespace
Contact & Contact \faCheck & --- & No change needed \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\vspace{0.8em}

\begin{yellowbox}
\textbf{\color{uvtDarkBlue}Rationale for critical corrections}

\vspace{6pt}
\color{textGray}
\textbf{\textit{``Admissions''}} is the wrong term. DRI does not run university
admissions. This section serves incoming international students. Using the wrong
label breaks their user journey from the landing page onward.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{\textit{``Research''}} does not correspond to any DRI function.
Partner institutions looking for bilateral agreements will skip past it.
This directly breaks the partner institution flow documented in Week~2.
\end{yellowbox}

\vspace{0.8em}

\textbf{Correct full top-level menu order:}

\vspace{0.4em}
\begin{bluebox}
About DRI \quad\faArrowRight\quad Erasmus+ \quad\faArrowRight\quad
International Students \quad\faArrowRight\quad Scholarships \& Exchanges
\quad\faArrowRight\quad Partnerships \quad\faArrowRight\quad News
\quad\faArrowRight\quad Contact
\end{bluebox}

\vspace{0.5em}
\texttt{Scholarships \& Exchanges} and \texttt{News} are currently missing
from \texttt{Navbar.tsx} and must be added as placeholder links.
