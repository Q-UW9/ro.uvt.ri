# FAQ — International Students

**Page purpose:** Answers the most common questions from prospective and current international students at UVT.

## Admission

**Can I study at UVT in English?**
Yes. UVT offers several degree programmes taught entirely in English, French, or German. The full list of programmes in foreign languages is available on the DRI website.

**What documents do I need to apply?**
Required documents vary by nationality and programme, but typically include your previous degree diploma and transcript, a copy of your passport, proof of language proficiency, and a medical certificate. Non-EU applicants may also need additional documents for the visa process.

**Is there an entrance exam?**
This depends on the faculty and programme. Some programmes require an interview or portfolio review. Contact the relevant faculty directly to confirm requirements.

## Visas and Residence

**Do I need a visa to study in Romania?**
EU and EEA citizens do not need a visa. Citizens of non-EU countries typically need a long-stay visa (type D) before arriving. DRI can provide a letter of acceptance to support your visa application.

**What do I do after I arrive regarding residence?**
Non-EU students must register with the Romanian Immigration Office (IGI) within 90 days of arrival to obtain a residence permit. DRI can guide you through the process.

## Life at UVT

**Is accommodation available for international students?**
UVT has student dormitories, but availability is limited. It is recommended to apply for accommodation as early as possible. DRI can also provide guidance on private housing options in Timișoara.

**Is there support available in English?**
Yes. DRI staff communicate in English and can assist with administrative matters. An orientation session for new international students is held at the start of each semester.

**Who do I contact if I have a problem during my studies?**
Contact DRI directly at international@e-uvt.ro or visit the office at the main campus building, 1st floor, room 159, during office hours (Monday–Friday, 10:00–14:00).
