# FAQ — Erasmus+

**Page purpose:** Answers the most common questions from students and staff about the Erasmus+ programme at UVT.

## For Students

**Who can apply for an Erasmus+ mobility?**
Any UVT student enrolled in a Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD programme can apply, provided they have completed at least one year of study. You must apply through your faculty's Erasmus+ coordinator during the official call period.

**How long can I study abroad with Erasmus+?**
Study mobilities last between 2 and 12 months per study cycle. Placements (traineeships) also last between 2 and 12 months. The total Erasmus+ mobility period across all cycles is capped at 12 months per cycle.

**Will my credits be recognised when I return?**
Yes. Credits earned at the host university are recognised by your faculty at UVT, provided they were agreed in your Learning Agreement before departure. Changes to the Learning Agreement must be approved by both institutions within 30 days of arrival.

**Do I need to speak the language of the host country?**
Not necessarily. Many partner universities offer courses in English. Your language proficiency must match the language of instruction at the host institution, and you may be asked to provide proof before departure.

**What is the Erasmus+ grant amount?**
Grant amounts vary by destination country and are published each call period. The grant is intended to help cover additional costs of living abroad and does not fully replace your regular income or scholarship.

## For Staff

**Who can apply for a teaching or training mobility?**
Any academic or administrative staff member employed at UVT can apply, subject to available funding and an active bilateral agreement with the destination institution.

**How do I find a host institution?**
Contact DRI to check the list of active Erasmus+ partner universities and organisations. You can also reach out directly to colleagues at partner institutions to arrange a visit.

**How far in advance should I apply?**
At least 4–6 weeks before your intended mobility start date to allow time for document preparation, grant agreement signing, and any visa requirements.
