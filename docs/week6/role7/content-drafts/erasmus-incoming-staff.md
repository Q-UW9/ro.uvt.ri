# Erasmus+ Incoming Staff

**Page purpose:** Guides academic and administrative staff from partner universities through completing a teaching or training mobility at UVT.

## Teach or Train at UVT
UVT welcomes academic and administrative staff from Erasmus+ partner institutions for teaching assignments (STA) and training visits (STT). Incoming staff mobilities contribute to knowledge exchange and strengthen the relationship between UVT and its partner universities.

## Teaching Mobility (STA)
Academic staff wishing to deliver lectures or seminars at UVT must be employed at a partner institution with an active Erasmus+ bilateral agreement with UVT. A minimum of 8 teaching hours per week of mobility is required. A Mobility Agreement for Teaching must be completed and signed before departure.

## Training Mobility (STT)
Administrative and academic staff can visit UVT for a training period to observe practices, attend workshops, or shadow colleagues. A Mobility Agreement for Training must be agreed upon with the relevant UVT department before the visit. Minimum duration is 2 working days.

## Practical Information
DRI will assist incoming staff with scheduling, campus access, and any administrative requirements. Staff are responsible for arranging their own travel and accommodation. Timișoara is well connected by air and rail to major European cities.
