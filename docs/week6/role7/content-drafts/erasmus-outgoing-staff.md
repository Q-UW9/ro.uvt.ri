# Erasmus+ Outgoing Staff

**Page purpose:** Guides UVT academic and administrative staff through applying for a teaching or training mobility abroad.

## Go Abroad with Erasmus+
UVT academic and administrative staff can apply for Erasmus+ grants to teach at a partner university (STA) or attend a training activity abroad (STT). Mobilities are funded through the university's Erasmus+ grant and must be completed within the active project period.

## Teaching Mobility (STA)
Academic staff can deliver lectures or seminars at a partner institution with an active Erasmus+ agreement with UVT. A minimum of 8 teaching hours per week is required. You must complete a Mobility Agreement for Teaching and obtain approval from your faculty dean before departure.

## Training Mobility (STT)
Staff can participate in job shadowing, training events, or study visits at partner institutions or companies abroad. A Mobility Agreement for Training must be prepared in advance. The minimum duration is 2 working days, and the activity must have a clear professional development purpose.

## How to Apply
Contact DRI to check available partner institutions and confirm the call period. Submit your application including the Mobility Agreement, your CV, and a short motivation statement. Grant amounts depend on the destination country and are published each call period.
