# Erasmus+ Outgoing Students

**Page purpose:** Guides UVT students through applying for and completing an Erasmus+ study or placement mobility abroad.

## Study or Work Abroad with Erasmus+
As a UVT student, you can apply for an Erasmus+ grant to study at a partner university or complete a work placement (traineeship) abroad. Mobilities typically last between 2 and 12 months and are financially supported through an Erasmus+ grant.

## How to Apply
Applications are managed through your faculty's Erasmus+ coordinator. Check the list of available partner universities, confirm eligibility with your faculty, and submit your application during the official call period. DRI publishes calls for applications each semester on the website and social media channels.

## What You Need
You will need a valid Learning Agreement signed by your faculty coordinator and the host institution before departure. You will also need proof of language proficiency, a transcript of records, and a completed grant agreement with DRI. All documents must be submitted before your mobility start date.

## After Your Mobility
On return, you must submit your final transcript from the host university, a participant report via the EU Survey platform, and any other documents required by your grant agreement. Credits earned abroad will be recognised by your faculty in accordance with the Learning Agreement.
