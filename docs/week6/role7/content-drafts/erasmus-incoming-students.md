# Erasmus+ Incoming Students

**Page purpose:** Guides incoming Erasmus+ students through everything they need to arrive and study at UVT.

## Welcome to UVT
The West University of Timișoara welcomes Erasmus+ students from partner universities across Europe and beyond. Timișoara is a dynamic, multicultural city and a great place to study, explore, and connect with students from around the world.

## Eligibility and Nomination
To study at UVT as an Erasmus+ student, you must be nominated by your home university. Your home institution's international office will contact DRI directly. Once nominated, you will receive instructions on how to complete your application through UVT's system.

## What to Prepare
Before arriving, you will need to arrange your Learning Agreement, academic transcript, and proof of language proficiency. You will also need to organise accommodation — DRI can provide guidance on available options in Timișoara. Health insurance valid in Romania is required for the duration of your stay.

## On Arrival
Upon arrival, you must register with DRI and complete the enrolment process at your host faculty. An orientation session is organised at the start of each semester to help incoming students get settled. You will receive your student card and access to university facilities after completing registration.
