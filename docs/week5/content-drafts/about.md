# About DRI

**Page purpose:** Introduce the Directorate of International Relations at UVT and its role in supporting international academic collaboration.

## What is DRI?
The Directorate of International Relations (DRI) is the office at West University of Timișoara responsible for coordinating international academic partnerships, student mobility, and institutional cooperation. It serves as the main point of contact for all international activities at UVT.

## Our Mission
DRI works to foster global connections for students, academic staff, and researchers at UVT. Through programs such as Erasmus+, bilateral agreements, and international partnerships, DRI supports the university's commitment to academic excellence and cross-border collaboration.

## What We Do
DRI manages incoming and outgoing student and staff mobility, coordinates institutional agreements with universities worldwide, and provides guidance on scholarships and exchange opportunities. It also oversees the integration of international students into UVT's academic community.
