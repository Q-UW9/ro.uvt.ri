# Partnerships

**Page purpose:** Present UVT's international institutional partnerships and invite potential partner universities and organizations to collaborate.

## Our Global Network
West University of Timișoara maintains active partnerships with universities, research institutions, and organizations across Europe and beyond. These agreements form the foundation for student and staff mobility, joint research projects, and academic cooperation at an international level.

## Types of Partnerships
DRI manages several categories of institutional agreements at UVT. Erasmus+ Inter-Institutional Agreements govern mobility with European partner universities. Bilateral cooperation agreements cover a broader range of academic collaboration with institutions outside the Erasmus+ program. DRI also supports faculty-level agreements coordinated through individual departments.

## Becoming a Partner
Universities and institutions interested in establishing a partnership with UVT are welcome to contact DRI directly. The process begins with an expression of interest by email, followed by a review of the proposed cooperation framework and the signing of a formal agreement.
