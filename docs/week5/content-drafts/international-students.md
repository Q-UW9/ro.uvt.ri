# International Students

**Page purpose:** Welcome international students to UVT and provide them with the key information they need to begin their studies in Timișoara.

## Welcome to UVT
West University of Timișoara welcomes students from around the world. Whether you are arriving through Erasmus+, a bilateral agreement, or as a degree-seeking student, DRI is here to support you before and during your stay in Timișoara.

## Before You Arrive
Once accepted, international students will receive guidance from DRI on visa requirements, accommodation options, and registration procedures. It is important to contact DRI as early as possible after receiving your acceptance letter to ensure all administrative steps are completed on time.

## When You Arrive
Upon arrival, international students are invited to attend the DRI orientation session held at the beginning of each semester. The session covers academic registration, campus facilities, student services, and life in Timișoara. A buddy program is available to pair new international students with local UVT students.
