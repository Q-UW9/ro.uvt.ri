# Erasmus+

**Page purpose:** Introduce the Erasmus+ program at UVT and guide students and staff toward the right mobility opportunity.

## What is Erasmus+?
Erasmus+ is the European Union's program for education, training, youth, and sport. At UVT, it is the primary pathway for students and staff to study, train, or teach abroad at partner universities across Europe and beyond. DRI coordinates all Erasmus+ mobility activities at the university level.

## Opportunities Available
UVT participates in Erasmus+ mobility for both incoming and outgoing participants. Students can apply for a semester or full academic year abroad at one of UVT's partner institutions. Academic and administrative staff can participate in teaching assignments or training mobility periods at partner universities.

## How to Apply
Applications for Erasmus+ mobility open each academic year through DRI. Eligibility requirements, partner university lists, grant amounts, and deadlines are published on this page and updated annually. Students should contact their faculty's Erasmus coordinator as a first step before applying through DRI.
