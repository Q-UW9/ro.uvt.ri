# Contact

**Page purpose:** Provide contact details and office information for the DRI so students, staff, and partners can reach the right person easily.

## Get in Touch
The Directorate of International Relations is located on the UVT campus in Timișoara. Our team is available during working hours to answer questions about international programs, mobility, partnerships, and student support.

## Office Details
- **Address:** Bd. Vasile Pârvan nr. 4, Timișoara, 300223, România
- **Email:** ri@e-uvt.ro
- **Phone:** +40 256 592 111
- **Office hours:** Monday – Friday, 09:00 – 16:00

## Who to Contact
For Erasmus+ and student mobility inquiries, contact the mobility coordinators directly through the email above. For institutional partnerships and cooperation agreements, contact the partnerships office. Staff and faculty should reference their faculty's international coordinator for department-specific questions.

## Find Us
DRI is part of the main UVT administrative building. Visitors are welcome during office hours. International students requiring in-person assistance are encouraged to book an appointment in advance by email.
