## Section 1: General Contact Information
The DRI office is located at West University of Timișoara, main building, 1st floor, room 155B, Blvd. V. Pârvan 4, Timișoara 300223, Timiș, România. The team is available Monday to Friday between 10:00 and 14:00 (GMT+3). For general inquiries, contact us by email at international@e-uvt.ro or by WhatsApp (calls only) at +40 755 240 243.

## Section 2: Erasmus+ Specific Contact
For questions specifically related to Erasmus+ mobility programs, the point of contact is Oana Ivan-Horobeț, Head of the European Cooperation Service. She can be reached by email at oana.ivan@e-uvt.ro or by phone at +40 256 592 372. Students and staff are encouraged to contact her directly for mobility application questions, grant details, and partner university information.

## Section 3: Stay Connected
DRI can also be reached through the official UVT international Facebook page. For updates on mobility calls, deadlines, and international events, follow DRI on social media or subscribe to the UVT newsletter. All official announcements regarding international programs are published on this website and through university communication channels.
