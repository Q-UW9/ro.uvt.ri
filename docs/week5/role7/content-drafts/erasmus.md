## Section 1: What is Erasmus+?
Erasmus+ is the European Union's program supporting education, training, youth, and sport through international mobility and cooperation. West University of Timișoara holds the Erasmus Charter for Higher Education 2021–2027 and actively participates in mobility programs with partner universities across Europe and beyond. UVT was also recognized as an ESCI Champion 2025–2027 by the European Commission.

## Section 2: Who Can Participate?
Erasmus+ at UVT is open to several groups within the university community:
- UVT students wishing to study or complete a traineeship abroad (outgoing)
- International students from partner universities coming to study at UVT (incoming)
- UVT academic and administrative staff wishing to teach or train abroad (outgoing)
- Staff from partner institutions coming to teach or train at UVT (incoming)
- Participants in mobility with Partner Countries outside the standard Erasmus+ programme area

## Section 3: Where to Start
Choose the sub-section that matches your situation. Each page contains eligibility requirements, application steps, deadlines, and grant information specific to that mobility type. If you are unsure where to begin, contact the Erasmus+ office directly at oana.ivan@e-uvt.ro.

## FAQ items (if applicable)
Q: How long can an Erasmus+ exchange last?
A: Mobility periods vary by type but typically range from 2 to 12 months for students and 5 days to 2 months for staff.
