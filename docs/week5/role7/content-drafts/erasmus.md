# Erasmus+

**Page purpose:** Landing page for the Erasmus+ programme at UVT, directing users to the relevant mobility sub-pages.

## About Erasmus+ at UVT
The West University of Timișoara participates in the Erasmus+ programme, enabling students and staff to take part in academic exchanges across Europe and partner countries. UVT holds the Erasmus Charter for Higher Education 2021–2027 and has been recognised as an ESCI Champion for 2025–2027.

## Mobility Options
Erasmus+ at UVT covers four types of mobility: outgoing students, incoming students, outgoing staff (teaching and training), and incoming staff (teaching and training). Each mobility type has its own application process, eligibility criteria, and grant conditions. Choose the category that applies to you below.

## Institutional Partners
UVT has bilateral Erasmus+ agreements with partner universities across Programme Countries and Partner Countries. A full interactive map of institutional partners is available through the DRI website.
