# International Students

**Page purpose:** Landing page for all prospective and current international students at UVT, with pathways based on their background.

## Study at UVT
The West University of Timișoara welcomes students from around the world. UVT offers degree programmes taught in foreign languages and provides dedicated support for international students throughout their studies. Timișoara is a multicultural city with a strong academic tradition and a vibrant student community.

## Who Is This For?
The information on this section is organised by student category. Select the path that matches your situation to find admission requirements, visa information, scholarship opportunities, and support services.
- Citizens of EU member states
- Citizens of non-EU countries (third-country nationals)
- Ukrainian citizens or students with studies in Ukraine
- Romanian citizens with degrees obtained abroad
- Free-movers
- Refugees and political asylum seekers

## Support and Resources
DRI provides guidance on admission, residence permits, the preparatory Romanian language year, and the "Eugen Ionescu" doctoral and postdoctoral scholarships. International students can also explore the Summer School programme and connect with the UVT international community through the International Stories platform.
