## Section 1: Welcome to UVT
West University of Timișoara welcomes students from around the world, regardless of their country of origin or the nature of their studies. This section is for all non-Romanian students considering or currently enrolled at UVT, including EU citizens, non-EU citizens, Ukrainian students, refugees, free movers, and students enrolled in degree programs taught in a foreign language. DRI is here to guide you through the admission process and support you throughout your stay in Timișoara.

## Section 2: What UVT Offers International Students
UVT offers a range of study programs taught in English and French, covering fields across the humanities, social sciences, natural sciences, and technology. International students have access to university support services including academic advising, library facilities, and student organizations. DRI coordinates a preparatory year in Romanian language for students who wish to study in Romanian, as well as scholarship opportunities for students from non-EU countries and the "Eugen Ionescu" doctoral and postdoctoral fellowships.

## Section 3: Where to Start
Select the category that best describes your situation to find the relevant admission requirements, visa information, scholarship options, and deadlines. If you are unsure which category applies to you, contact DRI at international@e-uvt.ro before applying.

## FAQ items (if applicable)
Q: Can I study at UVT in English?
A: Yes, UVT offers several degree programs taught entirely in English and French. A full list is available on the study programs page.

Q: I am from a non-EU country. Can I apply to UVT?
A: Yes. Non-EU students can apply through DRI. Requirements vary by country and program. Contact international@e-uvt.ro for guidance.

Q: What is the preparatory year?
A: The preparatory year is a Romanian language program for international students who wish to pursue a degree taught in Romanian but do not yet have the required language level.
