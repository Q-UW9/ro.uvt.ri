# Partnerships

**Page purpose:** Overview of UVT's international partnerships, agreements, and visiting academic opportunities.

## International Collaboration
The West University of Timișoara maintains an extensive network of international partnerships through bilateral cooperation agreements, Erasmus+ institutional agreements, and membership in global academic associations. DRI coordinates all incoming and outgoing partnership activities.

## Types of Partnerships
UVT engages in several forms of international collaboration: bilateral Memoranda of Understanding with universities worldwide, Erasmus+ bilateral agreements with Programme and Partner Country institutions, and affiliations with international academic associations. UVT is also a member of the UNITA European Universities Alliance.

## Visiting Academics and Lecturers
International academics are welcomed at UVT through the Visiting@UVT programme and through government or bilateral agreements. Staff planning international travel for academic purposes can submit a travel request through DRI.
