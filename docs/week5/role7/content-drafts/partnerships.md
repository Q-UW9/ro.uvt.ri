## Section 1: UVT's International Network
West University of Timișoara has signed bilateral collaboration agreements with universities and research institutions across more than 50 countries on six continents. Partner countries include France, Germany, Spain, Italy, China, the USA, Brazil, India, Japan, South Korea, and many more. These agreements form the foundation for student and staff exchanges, joint research initiatives, and participation in international academic events hosted at UVT or abroad.

## Section 2: Types of Partnerships
DRI manages several categories of international agreements on behalf of UVT. Erasmus+ Inter-Institutional Agreements govern student and staff mobility with European partner universities within the Erasmus+ programme. Bilateral Memoranda of Understanding cover broader academic cooperation with institutions outside the Erasmus+ area, including joint research, double-degree programs, and staff exchanges. DRI also manages UVT's affiliations with international university networks and associations, including the UNITA European University Alliance.

## Section 3: Proposing a New Partnership
Universities and institutions interested in establishing a collaboration agreement with UVT are welcome to contact DRI directly by email at international@e-uvt.ro. The process begins with an expression of interest, followed by a review of the proposed cooperation scope and the signing of a formal agreement coordinated through DRI and the relevant university leadership. Institutions hosting UVT visiting lecturers or wishing to send international lecturers to UVT should also contact DRI for guidance.

## FAQ items (if applicable)
Q: How many partner universities does UVT have?
A: UVT has bilateral agreements with institutions across more than 50 countries. The full list is available on the old site and will be migrated to this page.

Q: How do we start a partnership with UVT?
A: Send an expression of interest to international@e-uvt.ro. DRI will review the proposal and coordinate next steps with university leadership.

Q: Does UVT participate in the UNITA alliance?
A: Yes. UVT is an active member of the UNITA European University Alliance. DRI coordinates all UNITA-related activities within the university.
