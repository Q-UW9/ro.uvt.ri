## Section 1: What is the DRI?
The Directorate of International Relations (DRI) is the department at West University of Timișoara responsible for coordinating all international activities of the university. DRI integrates the international dimension across UVT's strategic priorities, managing everything from student mobility programs to institutional partnerships with universities worldwide. It operates under the university's broader internationalization strategy and works closely with all faculties.

## Section 2: What We Offer
DRI coordinates Erasmus+ mobility for both students and staff, manages bilateral agreements with partner institutions across more than 50 countries, and supports the recruitment and admission of international degree-seeking students. The directorate also facilitates UVT's participation in the UNITA European University Alliance and other international university networks. Whether you are a student looking to study abroad, a staff member interested in a teaching mobility, or an institution seeking a partnership, DRI is your starting point.

## Section 3: Where to Find Us
The DRI office is located at West University of Timișoara, main building, 1st floor, room 155B, Blvd. V. Pârvan 4, Timișoara. The team is available Monday to Friday, 10:00–14:00 (GMT+3). You can reach us by email at international@e-uvt.ro or by WhatsApp (calls only) at +40 755 240 243.
