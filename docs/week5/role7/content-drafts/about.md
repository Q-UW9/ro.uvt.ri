# About DRI

**Page purpose:** Introduces the Department of International Relations at UVT and its mission.

## What is DRI?
The Department of International Relations (DRI) at the West University of Timișoara coordinates all international academic activities. It is the central point of contact for students, staff, and partners seeking international collaboration opportunities. DRI operates under the university's broader strategy for internationalisation.

## Our Mission
DRI integrates the international dimension across all priority areas of UVT. It supports academic mobility, intercultural exchange, and global partnerships to position UVT as an internationally recognised institution. The department also coordinates UVT's membership in the UNITA European Universities Alliance.

## What We Do
DRI manages Erasmus+ mobility programmes, bilateral agreements, and the recruitment of international degree-seeking students. The team provides guidance and support to students, academic staff, and administrative personnel throughout every stage of their international experience.
